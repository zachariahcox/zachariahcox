# resources
recommended resources

## books
* staffeng.com -- required reading if you're at senior level, has a section with interviews of other staff engineers talking about how they changed their approach when they changed roles. what worked for them.
* [the managers's path](https://www.amazon.com/Managers-Path-Leaders-Navigating-Growth) -- excellent overview of the roles that exist in real engineering orgs
* crucial conversations -- practical guide to having stressful conversations. 
* tribal leadership -- interesting approach to team dynamics
* 7 habits of highly effective people -- recommended reading for everyone!
* [Radical Candor](https://www.amazon.com/dp/1250235375?lv=shuf&channelId=500&plpRedirect=mhFallback) -- practical advice for all kinds of leaders.
* [Designing data intensive applications](https://www.amazon.com/designing-data-intensive-applications/s?k=designing+data+intensive+applications) -- required reading for service engineers. Excellent overview of basic terms and approches.
* [Google SRE handbook](https://sre.google/sre-book/table-of-contents/)
